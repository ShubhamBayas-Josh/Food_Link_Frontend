- Every day, Pune generates nearly 1 million kg of food waste!

- Out of this, around 750,000 to 950,000 kg comes from households, messes, canteens, 
  and restaurants—food that could have fed thousands.

- Yet, thousands of people in Pune sleep hungry every night.

- India wastes around 68 million tonnes of food every year, enough to feed millions of people.




- The problem isn't food shortage—it’s food wastage and inefficient distribution.

- A huge quantity of food is thrown away daily while many people struggle to get even one meal.

- We need a bridge between food donors (hotels, messes, restaurants, households) and NGOs who can distribute it to the needy.




- That's where our platform comes in!

- An online platform connecting food donors and NGOs in real time.

- Simple, efficient, and impactful—donate surplus food, claim food, and reduce waste!




- Food donors list surplus food (with location & quantity).

- NGOs or volunteers claim and collect the food.

-Tracking system ensures food reaches the needy.

- Ensures proper coordination and minimizes wastage.
